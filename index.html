01   The loop

A contract arrives for review with a deadline on it. A licence for one of the subsidiaries quietly nears renewal. An employee case waits on a policy check nobody has an hour for. None of it is the judgement. All of it is the queue.

At group level, the work is coordination before it's legal. Contracts arrive in Arabic and English, across construction, leases, NDAs, joint ventures and tenders. Permits, registrations and licences each run their own clock across every entity. Employee cases need a policy check, a record and a letter. The judgement stays where it belongs — everything before and after it is admin.

03   How it works

Four gaps, in the order they usually hurt.

01

One register, one ladder

Every dated obligation across every entity in a single register. Then it remembers on its own:

90dOwner named
60dPapers requested
30dEscalated
7dNamed person

Nothing expires because it lived in one subsidiary's spreadsheet.

02

Contracts that start from a draft

First drafts and redlines come out of the group's own clause library. Two questions sort the queue before it reaches you — does it deviate from the standard position, and does it carry exposure the group doesn't normally accept. Your review starts at the exceptions.

03

The case file writes the report

One record per employee case: the policy checked, the stage, the letter, the close date. The weekly case report and monthly dashboard read from that record — so the report is a view, not a task.

04

Recruitment and company paperwork

CV screening, shortlists and interview slots for the open roles; resolutions, minutes and filings generated from templates and routed for signature.
